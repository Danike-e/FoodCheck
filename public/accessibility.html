Accessibility statement

Last updated 23 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use FoodCheck. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What you can do

  • Use every feature with a keyboard alone. The list of results gives the same information as the map, and selecting a business in the list highlights it on the map.
  • Use a screen reader. Ratings are announced in words, for example “Rating: 4 out of 5, Good”, and search progress and results are announced as they change.
  • Zoom in up to 400%, or make text bigger in your browser, without losing content.
  • Choose light or dark mode, or match your device, in Settings.
  • Expand the map to fill the screen with the button in its top-right corner. Press Escape, or the same button, to return.
  • Turn on “reduce motion” on your device, and FoodCheck’s animations will switch off.
  • Tell ratings apart without relying on colour: every badge and map pin also shows the number or a symbol, and each result explains the rating in words.

Known limitations

  • The map. Map images come from OpenStreetMap, so we can’t control their colours or text size. You can’t Tab to the pins on the map, so use the results list, which has the same information.
  • Press and hold to search. Picking a spot on the map needs a pointer or touch. You can always search by postcode or with Use my location instead.
  • Business details. Names and addresses are shown exactly as local authorities provide them, so some appear in capital letters.

Tell us about a problem

If you find something hard to use, or need information in a different format, contact us at [contact email]. Tell us the page, what you were trying to do, and the device and assistive technology you use. We’ll do our best to help.

How we tested

We last tested FoodCheck on 23 October 2026 with automated checks (axe-core, against WCAG 2.2 A and AA) and manual testing. The manual tests covered keyboard-only use, light and dark themes, phone and desktop screen sizes, and colour contrast.

Technical information

FoodCheck works with current versions of Safari, Chrome, Edge and Firefox on phones, tablets and computers. It is built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and needs JavaScript to search.
