FoodCheck is designed to know as little about you as possible. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising, and we never sell or share information about you.

What happens when you search

Searching by postcode

Your browser sends the postcode to postcodes.io to find its location. It then sends that location to the Food Standards Agency (FSA) ratings service to get nearby businesses. These requests go straight from your device to those services; we don’t see or keep them.

“Use my location”

Your location is only used if you tap Use my location and allow it when your browser asks. FoodCheck rounds your position to about 10 metres in your browser before sending it to postcodes.io (to find the nearest postcode) and to the FSA (to find nearby businesses). We never receive or store it.

So that results can be bookmarked or shared, the page address then contains your position rounded to about 100 metres. It will appear in your browser history and in any link you share. If you don’t want that, search by postcode instead.

Pressing and holding the map

The point you pick is handled the same way: it is sent to postcodes.io and the FSA to run the search, and it appears in the page address.

Sharing

Share buttons make their QR code on your device; nothing is sent to us. The link is only sent somewhere if you choose to: to the app you pick on your device, or to WhatsApp, Facebook or X if you use those buttons, which then handle it under their own privacy policies. A shared search made with Use my location includes your position rounded to about 100 metres, and FoodCheck tells you this before you share.

The map

Map images are loaded from OpenStreetMap’s tile servers, which can see which area of the map you are viewing.

Hosting

This website is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel’s servers process your IP address, browser details and the pages you request so they can deliver the site and protect it from abuse, and they keep short-lived technical logs. Occasionally the app sends a ratings search through our own server on Vercel instead of directly to the FSA. When it does, the search is passed on and the result is cached, but it isn’t linked to you.

What is stored on your device

FoodCheck doesn’t use cookies. To make the app faster and work offline, it keeps a few things in your browser’s storage. They are never sent to us:

  • your appearance choice (light, dark or match device), if you set one;
  • your search history (the places you searched and the filters you used, with any device location rounded to about 100 metres), unless you turn it off in Settings or on the History page;
  • whether you have seen the “press and hold the map” tip;
  • the list of business types used by the filter;
  • the app’s files and recent results, so FoodCheck still opens without a connection.

These are only used to provide features you ask for, so we don’t need to ask for consent. You can remove them at any time in Settings → Clear saved data, or by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Our legal basis

The only personal information that reaches us is the technical data our host processes, and it is used to run the website securely. We rely on our legitimate interest in doing that (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, and you can object to how we use it. In practice we hold almost nothing that could identify you, but contact us at [contact email] and we will help.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Children

FoodCheck is a general information service. It doesn’t ask for or knowingly collect information about anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how FoodCheck handles information, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
