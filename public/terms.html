The ratings information

  • Ratings come from the FSA’s public data, which local authorities supply. We show them as we receive them and don’t change them.
  • A rating reflects hygiene standards on the day of the inspection. Things may have improved or got worse since then, and a business may have appealed, asked for a re-inspection or published a right to reply.
  • Some businesses have no map location, or a location that is only approximate. Distances are straight-line estimates.
  • The information may be incomplete, delayed or wrong. Always check the official record on the FSA website (each listing links to it) before making a decision that matters.

FoodCheck is for general information only. It isn’t advice about whether a particular business is safe to eat at.
